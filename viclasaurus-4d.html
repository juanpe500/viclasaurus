To reach the break-out fast: click the strip to start, then — with the game focused — type sp to fast-forward the sim ~10×. In a few seconds you'll clear level 5 and burst onto this page. Then: ← → move, Space jump, Esc to bail out. (The sp turbo taints the run, so it won't set a hi-score — that's expected while testing.)

WHAT HAPPENS AT LEVEL 6

Instead of diving into THE DEEP, the runner launches up out of its little strip. A full-viewport overlay takes the whole screen, and every visible box on this page is scanned into a one-way platform — you can jump up through a ledge from below and land on it from above. Neon enemies home in from the edges; each hit knocks your health bar down. When it empties, the chaser's jaws swing shut and you drop back into the strip as GAME OVER.
