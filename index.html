USE IT

Point it at any div. Until the player clicks, the div contains nothing but a neon button with your text — zero rendering cost. The click loads the game and starts the first run immediately. 

// the div decides the size — the game covers it completely
const div = document.getElementById("strip");
const dino_game = game_instance(div, "Click me");

dino_game.score;      // live score (read-only)
dino_game.level;      // current level
dino_game.state;      // "waiting" | "run" | "over"
dino_game.restart();  // fresh run (boots the game if still waiting)
dino_game.destroy();  // remove everything, free every listener

SURVIVE THE CYCLE

Every level lasts exactly 12 seconds — only the speed climbs. Every 5 levels the game reinvents itself in a warp: new physics, new obstacles, new palette. After the fifth world the cycle restarts, faster, and the darkness starts following you.

THE DESERT LV 1–5

The classic. Tap to jump, hold to crouch, press mid-air to slam down. Cacti, birds at three altitudes — one of them only crouching survives.

THE DEEP LV 6–10

The land runs out and you dive. Open water, no floor — tap to stroke, hold to sink. Jellyfish bob, mines wait. Drift fully off screen and you're gone.

THE STORM LV 11–15

Jetpack physics: hold to thrust, release to fall. Laser gates with narrow gaps, and missiles that announce themselves with a blinking ! before they arrive.

THE CAVERN LV 16–20

Classic controls, but you only see a spotlight around you. Stalactites force crouches, bats hunt at head height, and the chaser's eye glints in the dark.

THE MOLTEN PATH LV 21–25

The floor itself betrays you — lava pits open in the ground. Fast-fall becomes a precision landing tool. Then the cycle begins again… darker.

THE CHASER ALWAYS

Hits don't kill you — they knock you back toward the jaws that eat every obstacle you dodge. Run clean to rebuild your lead. Reach the teeth and it's over.

BUILT FOR EMBEDDING

◆ TRANSPARENT

No background is ever painted. Whatever sits behind the div shows through — headers, footers, hero sections, 404 pages.

◆ POLITE KEYBOARD

Space and arrows are only captured while the pointer is over the game or it has focus. It never hijacks scrolling or typing on the host page.

◆ AUTO-PAUSE

Scrolled out of view? The loop stops completely — zero CPU, zero audio — and resumes exactly where it froze.

◆ LAZY

Until the button is clicked nothing exists but the button. Put ten strips on a page; you pay only for the ones people play.

◆ FAIR BY DESIGN

Every spawn is checked in arrival-time space, so a fast bird can never overlap a cactus into an unwinnable wall.

◆ PIXEL-TRUE

Tiny logical canvas, CSS-scaled with crisp edges. Real chunky pixels with a neon glow, at any strip size.

THE ASCENT

OPEN SKY — hold a side to steer, tap to dash-step. Birds, balloons, kites swinging on their strings.

THE TEMPEST — no brakes: you bounce between the walls and only choose the direction. Thunderheads telegraph, then fire. Mind the gusts.

HIGH ORBIT — zero-g inertia, tap to retro-brake. Satellites, mines, tumbling debris.

THE BELT — darkness. You get a spotlight, the rocks get everything else.

THE CORONA — solar flares erupt from the walls. Then the cycle repeats, darker.

Chain stardust, graze obstacles for bonuses, grab shields and magnets — and race the dashed BEST line to your own record.

THE DREAM, IN RINGS

THE GARDEN — hold to spin, tap to dash. Petals, seed pods that burst, briar rings.

THE CLOCKWORK — you revolve forever; tap to reverse. Clock hands sweep, chime rings close in.

THE STATIC — tap to teleport-hop 45°. Glitch blocks flicker into being, and ring gaps lie about where they are.

THE ABYSS — darkness, and some of the lights down here are anglerfish.

THE CROWN — flare beams erupt from the eye itself.

Chain motes, thread rings for graze bonuses, grab the hourglass to slow the whole dream — and when the eye stares, get out of the light. Between runs, it watches your cursor. Try it.

TESTING CHEATS

With the game focused, type jp to toggle immortality or sp to fast-forward the whole simulation. Both are displayed on screen for the entire run and any run that ever touched a cheat is permanently disqualified from setting a hi-score. Cheat openly or don't cheat at all.
